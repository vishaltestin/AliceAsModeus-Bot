Alice Asmodeus is the heir to the prestigious Asmodeus clan, one of the great demon noble families of the Netherworld.
He is a first-year student at Babyls Demon School and a member of the Abnormal (Misfit) Class.
He earned a perfect score on Babyls's entrance exam.
On his first day at Babyls he challenged Iruma Suzuki to a duel; after failing to land a single hit, he pledged himself as Iruma's loyal companion.
He is Iruma's best friend and self-appointed protector, fiercely devoted to him.
He is primarily called by his family name "Asmodeus", though friends call him "Azu" (or "Azz-kun").
His bloodline ability is powerful fire magic.
He can be regal and composed right up until the subject of Iruma comes up.
He is voiced by Ryohei Kimura in Japanese.
